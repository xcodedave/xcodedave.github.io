this site is for the BEST GAME EVER, Super Mario 64!!! its made by Shigeru Miyamoto, the same guy who made the original Mario and Zelda, so you know its good.
so heres the story. Princess Peach invited Mario to her castle for cake but Bowser got there first and locked her up, and he hid the Power Stars in the paintings!! you have to jump INTO the paintings to get to the courses. there are 120 stars in the whole game and I have gotten all of them (see My Records below). everything is in 3-D which is awsome, and you can run around any way you want with the analog stick.

The Courses
  • Bob-omb Battlefield - the first one. you fight a giant Bob-omb at the top of the mountain and ther is a Chain Chomp thats really scary the first time
  • Whomp's Fortress - big stone guys that fall on you. dont get squished!!
  • Jolly Roger Bay - a sunken ship and a eel. I always run out of air here
  • Cool, Cool Mountain - snow and a slide. you have to find the mother penguin's baby
  • Big Boo's Haunt - ghosts!!! I hate this one but the music is cool
  • Lethal Lava Land, Shifting Sand Land, Dire Dire Docks, Snowman's Land... and alot more. I will put them all up soon, i promise!!

Tips & Tricks
i figured these out myself (or my friends did) and I tried them all so they work. sorry about my spelling!!
  • 100 coin stars: in every course (not Bowsers) you get a star if you collect 100 coins, it pops up where you got the last one. red coins count as 2 and blue coins count as 5 so grab those!! Bob-omb Battlefield is the easiest one to start with. DONT die before you get it, the coin counter starts over if you leave the course
  • 8 red coins: ther are ALWAYS 8 of them for a red coin star. the star counter at the top tells you how many you have. if you cant find the last one look UP or behind stuff
  • The cannon: in Bob-omb Battlefield talk to the pink Bob-omb on the hill and he opens the cannon for you. to get the star on the island in the sky you aim it almost all the way up and shoot
  • Mips the rabbit: you need 15 stars before he shows up in the basement. he is SO fast. chase him into a corner and dive on him and he gives you a star. he comes back with a second star when you have 50 stars
  • Yoshi on the roof: get all 120 stars and go up on the roof of the castle (the cannon outside!!). Yoshi gives you 100 lives and a message, and I think you get the triple jump thing too but im not sure. thanks to mike_n64 for telling me how to get up there!!
  • Endless Stairs: you need 70 stars to open the door. the stairs go on furthur than you think until you run up them really fast, then you get to the top. dont fall!
  • The slide: Princess's Secret Slide is behind a painting in the castle. for the 2nd star you have to get to the bottom in under 21 seconds. dont bump the walls and use the shortcuts, my time is up above
  • Wing cap in the castle yard: when you have 10 stars look up at the sun spot out front and the wing cap switch is nearby. then fly around the castle grounds for a coin star. the camera is annoying so it takes a few tries
  • Bowser: grab his tail from behind and spin him around (turn the stick in a circle) then let go with B and he lands on the bombs. 3 hits to win!!
